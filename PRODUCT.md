# LaserSETI Product Brief

Status: Approved

## Product

LaserSETI is a public-facing portfolio and project website explaining the LaserSETI initiative and its work.

## Audience

The site serves two primary audiences:

- Members of the general public who may be new to LaserSETI, optical SETI, or the underlying science.
- Scientists who expect technically credible, precise information about the project.

Content should remain approachable to a general audience without sacrificing scientific substance or credibility.

## Primary goal

The primary goal is public education.

Visitors should leave with a clearer understanding of LaserSETI, what it does, and why the work matters.

## Primary action

The main call to action should direct visitors to the Help page.

## Voice

Writing should be:

- Optimistic
- Technical
- Accessible enough for non-specialists
- Credible for scientific readers

Technical concepts should be explained clearly rather than removed or oversimplified.

## Starting site map

1. Home
   - Keep the current homepage feature and content as-is for now.
   - Provide a clear route to the Help page.
2. Work
   - Retain this as a starting top-level page.
   - The specific work entries and page structure are still to be defined.
3. About
   - Feature a listing of the team.
4. Help
   - Serve as the destination for the site’s primary call to action.

There is no case-study page at this stage because the project currently has no case studies.

## Current constraints

- Do not redesign or reinterpret the current homepage feature yet.
- Do not invent case studies.
- No additional required pages have been identified.
- Visual references in `design/inspiration/` are inputs for a later phase and are not approved product or design direction.
- `DESIGN.md`, starter tokens, and starter visual defaults are not approved visual direction.

## Open product detail

- Define which project material belongs on the Work page when that content is ready.
